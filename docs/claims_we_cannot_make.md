# Claims We Cannot Make

MOSAIC RESCUE is a computational/preclinical research project. Scientific restraint is part of the design.

## Never claim

- that we found a cure for MVA
- that a nominated drug should be given to the patient
- that a computational score proves clinical benefit
- that a predicted future cellular state is a clinical prognosis
- that a persistent karyotype is automatically protective
- that an absent karyotype was necessarily eliminated by selection
- that DepMap cancer dependencies directly transfer to pediatric constitutional disease
- that transcriptomic reversal proves therapeutic efficacy
- that Cell Painting similarity proves mechanism
- that AlphaFold or pLDDT alone proves a mutation is destabilizing
- that a structural prediction proves a drug will rescue function
- that association within one patient establishes causality
- that increased survival of aneuploid cells is necessarily beneficial
- that pharmacogenomic relevance exists without a drug-gene relationship supported by authoritative guidance

## Preferred language

Use:

- “hypothesis”
- “candidate mechanism”
- “consistent with”
- “supports / weakens”
- “model-based forecast under stated assumptions”
- “preclinical computational evidence”
- “requires experimental validation”

Avoid:

- “proves” unless the evidence truly warrants it
- “will prevent”
- “will cure”
- “safe for this child”
- “predicts what will happen to the patient”

## Special MVA safety principle

A mechanism that improves tolerance of aneuploidy can be double-edged. It may reduce stress in some cells while also allowing genomically unstable cells to persist. Every rescue claim must therefore be paired with an explicit do-no-harm analysis focused on CIN, clonal fitness, proliferation, apoptosis, and tumor-relevant biology.
