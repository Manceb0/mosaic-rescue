# Crazy Ideas — High-risk, high-upside research transfers

This file is intentionally permissive at the idea stage, but every entry must eventually become testable.

## CI-01 — Ghost Karyotypes as Negative-Space Biology

**Question:** Could chromosomal states that are expected from the observed missegregation process but are depleted or absent reveal active negative selection?

**Unexpected transfer:** Structural engineering often infers hidden failure from deformation patterns and missing load-bearing behavior before a visible crack forms. Here, the “missing state” may be biologically informative.

**Needed:** a defensible expected-state model plus observed karyotype/mosaic data.

**Critical risk:** absence may reflect sampling, technical bias, tissue specificity, or generation probability rather than selection.

**Kill test:** if expected-state probabilities are poorly calibrated or depletion disappears after correcting for ascertainment, reject.

---

## CI-02 — Evolutionary Early Warning

**Question:** Can subtle distortions in mosaic-state frequencies act as early warning signals of a future shift in cellular population structure?

**Transfer:** ecosystem regime-shift forecasting / evolutionary dynamics.

**Needed:** ideally longitudinal single-cell or repeated mosaic measurements; otherwise only simulation under assumptions.

**Do not claim:** prediction of the patient’s clinical future.

---

## CI-03 — Natural Gene Therapy Analogy

**Question:** Could MVA contain functional rescue states analogous to somatic reversion/rescue mosaicism seen in other genetic diseases?

**Idea:** not necessarily true genetic reversion; instead, secondary chromosomal/dosage states may partially compensate for the primary defect.

**Needed:** within-patient persistence signal + independent suppressor evidence.

---

## CI-04 — Rescue Replay Toward the Patient’s Best State

**Question:** Instead of comparing candidate drugs against an average healthy signature, can we ask whether they reproduce the molecular state of the patient’s own best-performing cells?

**Needed:** a measurable rescue-state signature and perturbational drug signatures.

**Kill test:** if the “best state” is not robustly defined or is itself oncologically unsafe, reject.

---

## CI-05 — Safe Rescue Corridor

**Question:** Can candidate interventions be ranked on a multi-objective trajectory: improve cellular function while avoiding increased CIN, abnormal clone fitness, apoptosis escape, or proliferation?

**Key principle:** rescue ≠ survival.

---

## CI-06 — Cell Competition as Hidden Mechanism

**Question:** Are persistent aneuploid states intrinsically fitter, or do they persist because cellular competition rules changed?

**Needed:** evidence tied to MYC/p53 or other competition mechanisms, preferably with lineage/context matching.

---

## CI-07 — Structural Rescue Router

**Question:** Can structural modeling distinguish whether a patient variant is best framed as dose loss, instability, interface disruption, catalytic loss, or another mechanism — and therefore change which therapeutic strategy is even plausible?

**Allowed tools:** experimental structures first when available; AlphaFold/AlphaFold 3, FoldX, Rosetta, conservation and interface metrics as hypothesis-generating evidence.

**Do not do:** mutant-vs-WT pLDDT comparison as proof of pathogenic mechanism.

---

## CI-08 — Cross-Disease Suppressor Transfer

**Question:** Can suppressors discovered in cancer, yeast, neurodegeneration, mitochondrial stress, or aneuploidy models be transferred into MVA only when the same mechanistic bottleneck is demonstrated?

**Goal:** find a mechanism that nobody would search for under the label “MVA” but becomes obvious when searching by mechanism.

---

## CI-09 — Next Best Experiment Engine

**Question:** Given competing rescue hypotheses, can we estimate which experiment would produce the largest expected reduction in uncertainty?

**Transfer:** active learning / optimal experimental design.

**Output:** not just “our best hypothesis,” but “the exact next experiment scientists should run.”

---

## CI-10 — General Rare-Disease Mechanism Transfer Engine

**Question:** Can the MVA pipeline generalize to other disorders where mosaicism, spontaneous rescue, dosage shifts, or clonal selection expose hidden biological compensation?

**Constraint:** MVA remains the primary use case; scalability is demonstrated by mechanism abstraction, not by vague platform claims.

---

## Submission rule for new crazy ideas

Every new entry must eventually answer:

```text
What is the strange observation?
→ What hidden mechanism could explain it?
→ What measurable prediction follows?
→ What independent evidence could validate it?
→ What result kills the idea?
```
