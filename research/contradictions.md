# Contradictions Registry

This file is for evidence that weakens, complicates, or falsifies our preferred ideas.

## Why this exists

A hypothesis that survives contradiction is more valuable than one supported only by cherry-picked literature.

## Required questions

- Does this contradiction attack the observation, mechanism, generalizability, or safety claim?
- Is the contradictory evidence from the same biological context?
- Can both findings be true under different tissues, doses, developmental stages, or karyotypes?
- What new experiment would resolve the conflict?
