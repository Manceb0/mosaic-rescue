## Rules

- Prefer primary literature.
- Distinguish patient-specific evidence from external model-system evidence.
- Do not collapse cancer-cell findings into pediatric constitutional disease without context.
- Add negative or contradictory evidence with equal priority.
- For drug-related evidence, record approval status, mechanism, tissue relevance, pediatric context, and known risk where available.
